Snowball vs. Avalanche — Which Should You Choose?

❄️ The Debt Snowball

Pay minimums on everything, then attack the smallest balance first. When it's gone, roll its payment onto the next-smallest balance — the payment "snowballs" as each debt disappears.

Why people love it: quick wins. Knocking out whole accounts early keeps you motivated, and studies show people who see progress fast are more likely to stick with the plan. This is the method made famous by Dave Ramsey.

⛰ The Debt Avalanche

Pay minimums on everything, then attack the highest interest rate first. When it's gone, roll its payment onto the next-highest rate.

Why people love it: it's the mathematically optimal order. Because your most expensive debt dies first, the avalanche always pays the least total interest and is never slower than the snowball.

The honest answer

The best method is the one you'll actually finish. The comparison tab above shows the exact dollar-and-month difference for your debts — for many people it's small, and the motivation from snowball wins are worth more than the interest saved by the avalanche. If the difference is large, the avalanche may be worth the discipline.

Either way, the biggest lever is the extra payment — try changing it above and watch both dates move.
